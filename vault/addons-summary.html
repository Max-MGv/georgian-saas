Add-ons and wine for the table

What we're building, what changes for whom, the order of work, and five things I need you to confirm. If anything looks wrong, comment on it here or reply in chat. The detailed version for building is vault/Plan-AddOnsAndWine.md.

What we're building

Add-ons

Masterclass becomes one group of add-ons. You create others (Food, …), name them in English and Georgian, and choose who sees each group and for which visit type.

Wine for the table

Guests add bottles when booking, or staff add them later from the booking page. The bottles go on the bill and count as wine sold, in the same place as every other bottle.

Guest purchases

A guest buying wine for themselves gets their own pickup order, linked to the visit so you can see what the visit brought in. It never goes on the company's invoice.

What changes for whom

Guests

  • One row per add-on group on the booking form, collapsed by default like the masterclass row today.
  • "Per person" items are multiplied by the number of guests automatically. Flat fees have no quantity box.
  • A "Wine for the table" row with +/− per bottle. Companies see their own wine prices once their code is accepted.
  • Private guests get add-ons too, if you switch a group on for them.

Admin setup

  • Admin → Add-ons (renamed from Masterclass): groups, items, names in both languages, who sees what. Your existing masterclass items move into a "Masterclass" group automatically.
  • Wines: an "Offer on bookings" switch per wine.
  • Companies: booking companies get a "Table wine prices" section (a % and/or agreed prices). Empty means website prices.

Booking page

  • Cards for Add-ons, Wine for the table, Extras, and Sales from this visit (not on the invoice).
  • Add wine during the dinner. If it's added after payment, a balance due appears and the existing payment link works. If it's removed after payment, a credit shows.
  • Change the guest count and per-person add-ons follow, noted in the booking's history.
  • The staff booking sheet lists add-ons and table wine.

Invoice

  • Sections: visit · each add-on group · wine for the table · extras. Each wine line shows its price and why ("agreed", "−10%").
  • Invoices already sent stay exactly as they were.

Statistics

  • Wine: one view of every bottle sold, with a source filter: wine orders / table wine / guest purchases. Table wine counts on the visit day.
  • Bookings: a switch between visit only · + table wine · + guest purchases.
  • Fixes on the way: one wine no longer splits in two by language, and per-wine revenue includes discounts.

Edge cases, and what happens

A wine or add-on is renamed, switched off or deleted after it was bookedThe booking and its invoice keep the name and price it was sold at. It just disappears from the form.
The visit type changes after add-ons were pickedOn the form, add-ons that no longer fit are cleared. On the admin page they stay, with a warning, and staff decide.
A booking is cancelledIts table wine leaves the statistics with it. Guest purchases are their own orders and stay.
A company booking has no price yet ("confirmed after submission")Same as today. The total waits for staff to set the rates and no card payment is taken. The invoice can't show a negative visit amount any more.
Wine module switched offNo wine on the form or in setup. Past table wine still shows on its bookings and invoices.
Booking revenue + wine revenueThey overlap on purpose (table wine is in both), so nothing will ever add the two together.
Nikalas Marani sees the changeThe menu says "Add-ons" instead of "Masterclass", and per-person items on the public form are now charged per guest, as the admin screen always said. Both go in the release note.

Not in this plan

  • Guests linking their own later online orders to a visit. Only purchases recorded at the winery are supported.
  • Stock counts. Keeping all wine in one table makes this easy to add later.
  • A refund flow. Credits are settled by hand, as today.
  • Changes to extras. They stay as they are.
  • Table wine on the packing list.
