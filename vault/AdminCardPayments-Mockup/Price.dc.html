6 · “Set the price first” — when marking paid is refused

A booking with no price (or 0 ₾) cannot be marked Paid by any method — Bank, Cash or Card — and the server refuses it. Today the screens swallow that kind of refusal and keep showing “Paid” until a refresh. The fix is one shared notice in the admin’s existing red style: the pill snaps back to what it was and the reason is written out. Dismissible; it also covers the errors that fail silently today (“Order not found”, “Failed to update status”).
