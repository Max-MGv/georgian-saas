9 · Decisions for you

Nothing is built until you answer. Reply in chat in any form — for example “1 B, 2–8 as recommended, 7 change …”. Each item says what I recommend and what changes if you pick otherwise.

1 · How “Card” looks in the menu (board 1) Recommend B

B — outlined, with “Guest pays now — link or QR”. Bank and Cash record money already received; Card only asks, so it should not look like a third “done” button. A (same green) is simpler but invites the wrong click.

2 · The popup (boards 3 and 4) Recommend as shown

Full amount owed, not editable; QR + Copy + Email + Open; “valid until”; switches to Paid by itself within seconds of Flitt confirming; closing it cancels nothing. Email appears only when the booking has an email address; none on wine orders for now (T-322).

3 · Bank / Cash while a link is open (board 2) Recommend as shown

Warn first and make you confirm (“Record cash anyway”); “Card” there reopens the same link. A pill mark shows a link is waiting. Alternative: only the pill mark, no warning — less in the way, but the double-charge risk is then silent.

4 · Card money on a cancelled order (board 5) Recommend as shown

An amber notice on the order page and the wine card only, worked out from the payments (so it also works for old orders). Not in the lists. Ships together with the settlement rule — otherwise late money is recorded and invisible.

5 · “Set the price first” (board 6) Recommend as shown

One shared red, dismissible notice; the pill snaps back; same notice for errors that are swallowed today. Not proposed (say so if you want it): greying out Bank / Cash / Card in advance when there is no price.

6 · “Nothing to pay” (board 7) Recommend as shown

Grey pill, a fourth payment category outside Unpaid / Invoice sent / Paid, no Paid or Invoice step in its menu; old 0 ₾ bookings already marked Paid stay Paid.
