No mockup needed

Only one sensible way to do these

  • Wines admin: an "Offer on bookings" switch on each wine (on each vintage if the tenant shows vintages).
  • Companies tab: a switch "Use this company's wine prices for table wine". The existing % and agreed-price section also appears for booking companies when it's on.
  • Admin → Add-ons (renamed from Masterclass): groups listed in order, each with its English/Georgian name and who sees it, and its items underneath. Masterclass becomes the first group automatically. "Wine for the table" appears as one group you can rename, move or switch off.
