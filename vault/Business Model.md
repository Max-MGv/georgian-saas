# Business Model

## The Options

| Model | Description | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly SaaS | Client pays recurring fee (e.g. 50 GEL/mo) | Predictable revenue | Need 20+ clients to matter |
| Setup + monthly | One-time onboarding fee + smaller recurring | Easier first sale, immediate revenue | Setup fee can feel like a barrier |
| Free 3 months → monthly | No cost to start, then recurring | Lowers adoption friction | Delays revenue, some won't convert |
| Per-booking commission | % of each booking value | Aligns with client success | Hard to track, invasive |

## Recommendation

**150–200 GEL one-time setup + 50 GEL/month**

- Setup fee: covers your time to deploy the instance, configure their branding, migrate data
- Monthly: recurring revenue, positions it as ongoing service not a one-time favor
- 50 GEL ≈ ~$18 USD — low enough that any active business covers it in one booking

Break-even math: 10 clients = 500 GEL/month (~$180). 20 clients = 1,000 GEL/month (~$360). Meaningful side income, could scale.

## What to Offer Clients

### What they get
- Public booking page (can embed or link from their site)
- Admin panel: orders, prices, companies, statistics
- 1 admin user account
- Setup and onboarding support

### What's not included (upsell later)
- Additional admin users
- Custom domain setup (or include for first year)
- Email notifications
- Data export / reports
